### ⬛ SYSTEM.BIO

I am an **Electronics & Communication Engineering student at UEM Kolkata**, driven by a singular focus: **building intelligent systems at the intersection of electronics, robotics, drones, and AI.** I specialize in rapid prototyping—from designing bare-metal microcontrollers and PCBs to developing autonomous drones and cloud-connected IoT solutions.

My engineering philosophy is built on **performance and real-world application.** Whether I'm building an AI-driven aerial platform like *Hexa-Agro* for precision pest management, or exploring GPS-denied autonomous flight, I build systems that solve tangible problems under strict constraints.

<br/>

### 🚁 DEPLOYED.SYSTEMS & RESEARCH

- 🌾 **AgroRover** — Autonomous ultrasonic pest-repellent and smart-irrigation rover for sustainable agriculture.
- 🚁 **Hexa-Agro** — AI-driven aerial platform for precision pest management using computer vision.
- 🛟 **Autonomous Disaster-Response Drones** — Autonomous UAV systems for surveying disaster areas and supply delivery.
- 🛰️ **GPS-denied Autonomous Drone** — Exploring navigation and autonomous flight without relying on GPS.
